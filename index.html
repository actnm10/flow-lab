MODEL & ACCURACY

A real solver, at browser scale.

A three-dimensional D3Q19 lattice-Boltzmann method evolves velocity and pressure. Viscosity, inertia and no-slip bounce-back at the voxelized object determine the flow. Streamlines are integrated through the current solved field; tracers move with that field.

The open wind tunnel uses a uniform inlet, far-field side boundaries and a damped outflow. Gravity is balanced by hydrostatic pressure in a constant-density fluid; there is no free surface, buoyancy or falling liquid.

This is an exploratory simulation, not a validated engineering tool. Coarse curved boundaries, finite domain size, weak compressibility and under-resolved wakes limit accuracy. Increase quality and compare results. A low-Mach stability limit may raise the effective viscosity; the Reynolds number always uses the effective value.

Blender import

Run the included launcher for .blend import. Installed Blender evaluates the active scene's visible meshes, modifiers, curves and instances at its saved frame. The longest bounding-box dimension becomes the object diameter. Closed, watertight objects give the best result. Details below a grid cell cannot be resolved. Embedded scripts are disabled. No files are sent to the internet.

Pressure views color the surface using pressure sampled just outside the boundary, relative to the far-field reference. Static + gravity includes the hydrostatic term −ρgy; it is not stagnation pressure. Other views use a neutral surface. Fluid density is 1.225 kg/m³. All vorticity colors use the magnitude of curl of the solved velocity. Tracer density changes visualization only.
